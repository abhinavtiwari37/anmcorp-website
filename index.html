Who We Are

Execution expertise built on real operational experience.

A & M Corporation supports organizations delivering government-supported skill development initiatives with structured, milestone-oriented project execution.

With more than 12 years of experience in DDU-GKY and other skilling projects, A & M Corporation understands the operational requirements, coordination needs and implementation challenges involved in delivering projects on the ground.

We work alongside Project Implementing Agencies (PIAs), training teams, employers and relevant stakeholders to coordinate activities throughout the project lifecycle — from establishing centre readiness and managing training operations to placement coordination, post-placement tracking and documentation follow-up.

Our focus is on dependable execution, clear reporting and consistent coordination, helping partner organizations manage project activities systematically.

Our Vision

To be a trusted nationwide execution partner for skill development initiatives that create sustainable employment opportunities and strengthen India's workforce.

Our Mission

To deliver structured project execution, support PIAs in achieving operational milestones, strengthen training and placement processes, and facilitate systematic documentation and coordination.
